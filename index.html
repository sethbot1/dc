      Pay table

      Your bet is split across 5 lines: the 3 rows and both diagonals. Pays are per line, times the line bet. The Dueck face is wild and doubles any win it helps make. This machine pays back about 95% over time, just like a real one.

      The shoe

      Table rules

      Decks in the shoe
      Dealer on soft 17

      A real shoe: every card is dealt from shuffled decks and leaves the shoe until the cut card comes out, so card counting really works. Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Double on any two cards, also after a split. Split up to 4 hands (aces get one card each). Late surrender. Insurance and even money pay 2 to 1. Rule changes take effect on the next shuffle.

      Pick a bet size, then press an odds button to bet. Win: your horse finishes 1st. Place: 1st or 2nd. Show: top 3. The number is what you get back per Dueck bet.

        What it pays

        A single-zero wheel with all 37 pockets in their real order. The ball's pocket is drawn fairly the moment you spin. When the ball lands on 0, every even-money bet (red, black, odd, even, 1–18, 19–36) gets half back (la partage), so the house edge is just 1.35% on those and 2.7% on the rest.
